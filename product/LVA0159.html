Reusable Silicone Straws With Brush & Carrying Case

Item#LVA0159

Product Details

Enjoy a healthy alternative to disposable straws with our Collapsable Silicone Straw set. Each reusable silicone straw comes with its individual carrying case and cleaning brush, making it easy for on-the-go travel. Whether you are having a party, a picnic, or a family trip, take these reusable straws with you and your friends to enjoy a plastic-free environment. Each straw is constructed with 100% BPA-free, food-grade silicone and has been lab-tested to ensure quality and safety for you and your family. Soft enough to bite down on, yet extremely durable, the silicone straws can withstand hot drinks or cold beverages. Each straw is chew-resistant making them perfect for children as well.
